Install

Apple Mail on iPad or iPhone

  1. Tap Copy signature above.
  2. Open Settings, then Apps, then Mail, then Signature.
  3. Choose Per Account, clear the box under the Consilium account, and paste.
If the formatting drops after pasting, tap Undo straight away (iPad keyboard Undo arrow, or shake an iPhone and tap Undo). Apple Mail restores the bold and colours.

Hostinger webmail

  1. Tap Copy signature above.
  2. In webmail, open Settings, then Signature.
  3. Paste into the signature box and save. If the editor has an HTML or source view, use Copy HTML code and paste that instead.
