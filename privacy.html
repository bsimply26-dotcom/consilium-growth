Privacy and cookies

Last updated 2 November 2026.

This website is run by Consilium Growth Project Management Services, trading as Consilium Growth, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Contact us at hello@consiliumgrowth.com.

What we collect

When you use the contact form we collect your name, company, email address, phone number if you give it, and your message. We use these only to reply to your enquiry and to follow up on it.

How the form works

Form submissions are processed by Formspree, which sends them to our email inbox. We do not sell or share your details with anyone else.

Cookies

With your permission, we use cookies from Google Analytics, Google Ads, LinkedIn and Meta to understand how the site is used and to measure our advertising. These only load if you click Accept on the cookie banner. If you decline, none of them load.

To change your choice, clear this site's cookies and storage in your browser and the banner will appear again.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to respond and manage any work that follows, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@consiliumgrowth.com and we will deal with it.
